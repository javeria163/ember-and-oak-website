Our Story

Twelve years, one flame that never went out.

How it started

Ember & Oak opened in a converted rail warehouse with a single wood-fired hearth and twelve seats. There was no gas line in the building, so the first menu was built entirely around what a live flame could do — and that limitation became the whole identity of the restaurant.

Today we still cook everything the same way: over oak, with patience, one dish at a time. The dining room grew, but the fire didn't change.

Meet the Chef

Marco Elner, Head Chef

Marco trained in wood-fire kitchens across three countries before settling here. He treats the fire itself as an ingredient — adjusting wood type and flame height the way another chef might adjust a spice.

"You can't rush a fire, and you can't fake one either. Every plate tells you if you tried."

What we hold onto

Principle What it means at our table
Fire only No gas, no microwaves, no shortcuts — every hot dish meets open flame.
Local first Ingredients sourced from farms within 50 miles whenever the season allows.
Seasonal honesty If it's not in season, it's not on the menu — no matter how popular it was last time.
Small batches We'd rather run out of a dish than serve it below standard.
